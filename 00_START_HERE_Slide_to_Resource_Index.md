# The New Design Surface — slide resource index

Yvonne Doll · Updated October 3, 2026

Choose the slide, then the action you want help with. Each main guide works on its own: open an approved assistant, attach the whole file or follow its copy-paste instructions, and provide the approved context it asks for. Markdown files open in a text editor; no installation is required for their portable prompts. The two TXT prompts include their own quick start.

Slide numbers follow the 17-slide presentation beginning with A.I. / Abstract Imagery. Resource numbers in older notes are legacy IDs, not slide numbers. Use the slide title as well as the number if you reorder the deck.

## Main downloads

- [4 · Co-authorship](Slide_04_Coauthorship_Find_Routes_Through_Pushback.txt) — Clarify the shared outcome, understand objections, and draft alternative routes and a collaborative note.
- [5 · There is no handoff](Slide_05_No_Handoff_Prepare_a_Useful_Review_Request.md) — Turn a rough concern into a focused question a colleague can answer.
- [6 · Volume is a lousy goal; 7 · Speed + Quality?](Slides_06-07_Review_Customer_Impact_Rework_and_HEART_Outcomes.md) — Choose the volume-impact route for customer benefit, rework and downstream effort, or the HEART route for release learning.
- [7 · Speed + Quality?; 15 · Remember to ask the right questions](Slides_07-15_Check_Flow_Risks_Outcome_Assumptions_and_Segments.md) — Review usability, the feature-to-outcome assumptions, and evidence about different users; or start with new-project questions.
- [8 · The Taste Trap · optional, no slide QR](Slide_08_Taste_Trap_Explain_Design_Choices.md) — Replace unexplained preferences with specific concerns, evidence, and decisions.
- [11 · Give AI the context your team already has](Slide_11_Build_a_Shared_Context_Brief.md) — Bring the problem, people, evidence, constraints and success conditions into a linked brief.
- [12 · When “we suspect” becomes “we know”](Slide_12_Audit_Claims_Carry_Evidence_and_Build_a_Decision_Brief.md) — Audit claims; optionally draft evidence records, project instructions and acceptance criteria, or build a full decision brief.
- [13 · More time with your customers](Slide_13_Plan_User_Research_and_Conversations.md) — Prepare learning questions, a proposed method, and a neutral conversation guide for human review.
- [14 · Screens and flows. Now, the AI’s behavior too.](Slide_14_Plan_UI_and_AI_Behavior_Around_Available_Data.txt) — Map data needs to available sources, gaps, permissions and UI or AI behavior choices.
- [16 · My working system · optional](Slide_16_Optional_Define_Iteration_Purpose_and_Readiness.md) — Clarify what this iteration is for and what readiness means.
- [16 · My working system · optional](Slide_16_Optional_Review_Decision_History.md) — Review prior decisions, their evidence and what has changed.
- 16 · Adjacent work, and 16–17 · Build your own working system — those two guides are not in this download. Use the purpose and decision-history guides for slide 16.

## Which route goes on which slide?

- Slide 4: use the new co-authorship prompt for pushback and alternative routes. The decision-brief route in the slide 12 guide is optional supporting material.
- Slide 6: use the volume-impact route in the slides 6–7 guide. It starts with what should improve for the customer, then reviews evidence, rework and who absorbed effort.
- Slide 7: link both the slides 7–15 risk review and the slides 6–7 HEART release review.
- Slide 8: the phrase guide remains available in the kit; no QR is required on the slide.
- Slide 12: use the claim-audit route and optional evidence carry-forward drafts. Drafting instructions does not configure a repository or enforce behavior.
- Slide 14: use the new data-planning prompt. The optional iteration-purpose guide is narrower and is not a substitute.
- Slide 15: use the same risk-and-flow guide as slide 7, choosing new-project questions or an existing-flow review.
- Slide 16: combines the included guides as needed rather than making them sequential steps. Those guides cover purpose and decision history. An adjacent-work guide is not in this download.
- Slide 17: share the full kit. A separate working-system exercise file is not in this download.

## Before using any result

Only share material approved for your assistant and audience. A download does not create access, install connectors, send messages, change project records or run a background process. Review sources, uncertainty and proposed actions with the responsible people. Native skill installation and tool permissions depend on your host; use the portable prompt when no supported installation is available.

Superseded drafts are not included in this repository. Supporting folders use the names in the links above so native setup instructions remain valid.
